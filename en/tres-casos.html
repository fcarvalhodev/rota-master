Parallel 1 · Master, INSS, Lulinha

Three cases,
not one

On social media, the Master case, the INSS fraud and the investigations into Lula's son appear as a single scandal. They are three different investigations, with different targets, amounts and periods. This page shows each one and the few points where they touch.

Side by side

Master case

What it is
Fraud at Daniel Vorcaro's Banco Master, and the influence network built to save the bank.
Operation
Compliance Zero, by the Federal Police. First phase in November 2025.
Amounts
R$40.6 billion in debts left behind; a hole of up to R$47 billion at the FGC.
Supreme Court rapporteur
André Mendonça, since February 2026.
Targets and people cited
Vorcaro, the former BRB president, Central Bank employees, Ciro Nogueira, Jaques Wagner. Flávio Bolsonaro is the subject of an inquiry on "Dark Horse". Supreme Court justices appear in reports.
Link to Lula
Meeting with Vorcaro in December 2024. We found no public investigation against Lula in the sources cited as of this review.
Link to Lulinha
None in the case files.

INSS fraud

What it is
Associations deducted monthly fees from retirees' pensions without authorization. 97% of those interviewed by the CGU said they had not authorized it.
Operation
Sem Desconto, by the Federal Police and the CGU, on April 23, 2025. Lula fired the INSS president the same day.
Amounts
R$6.3 billion deducted from 2019 to 2024, legal and illegal.
Supreme Court rapporteur
André Mendonça.
Targets and people cited
Associations, former INSS officials and lobbyist Antônio Carlos Camilo Antunes, known as "Careca do INSS".
Link to Vorcaro
None in the deductions. Master appears through another product, payroll loans.
Link to Lulinha
Cited for being a friend of a businesswoman who received money from Careca. The Federal Police says there is no sign of his direct participation in the fraud.

Lulinha inquiries

What it is
Suspicions of influence peddling by Fábio Luís Lula da Silva in his father's government.
Opened
July 30 and 31, 2026 (Mendonça) and August 4, 2026 (Dino).
Amounts
The Federal Police is investigating five transfers of R$300,000 from Careca to the company of a friend of Lulinha's. No amount is attributed to him.
Rapporteurs
Mendonça in two, Flávio Dino in one.
What they investigate
Medical cannabis at the Health Ministry, contracts with Dataprev and the use of public bodies to favor companies.
Link to Vorcaro
None in the case files.
Status
Ongoing. Lula says his son will be cleared and that, if he is guilty, he will have to pay.

Where they intersect

The points of contact are real, but narrow. None of them links Lulinha to Vorcaro.

Master × INSS

Master's payroll loans

Master lent to retirees with payments deducted from their pensions. Between January 2023 and November 2025, that came to about R$701 million. The INSS stopped renewing the agreement in October 2025 and suspended 250,000 contracts for lack of proof that the retirees had agreed. That is why Vorcaro testified at the INSS CPMI in February 2026. It is a different product from the association deductions.

Master × INSS × Lulinha

The same rapporteur at the Supreme Court

Justice Mendonça oversees the Compliance Zero and Sem Desconto operations and authorized two inquiries into Lulinha. A Federal Police report cited by Moraes says documents prepared for Mendonça gathered references to Lulinha without proof and treated those investigated unequally depending on their political side. Moraes asked for Mendonça to be investigated for this. It is an ongoing dispute inside the Supreme Court.

INSS × Lulinha

Careca and Lulinha's friend

The Federal Police is investigating five transfers of R$300,000 from a consultancy of Careca do INSS to the company of Roberta Luchsinger, a friend of Lulinha's. Her defense says she never had anything to do with the INSS deductions. The Federal Police says it cannot prove she knew where the money came from. Lulinha is not a formal target in the fraud inquiry.

The deductions year by year

Amounts deducted by associations from pensions, according to the CGU. The fraud did not begin under a single government, but the volume exploded from 2023 on.
