Parallel 3 · Lagoinha Baptist Church

The Lagoinha
branch

The money and people of the Master case passed through a Lagoinha congregation in Belo Horizonte: the pastor brother-in-law, the pastor sister, the converted father and the church's leader, a family friend for 30 years. This page shows the money trail, who is who and how deputy Nikolas Ferreira enters the story.

The church as an institution is not under investigation. What is in the reports are people linked to it and the account of one congregation. Millions of people attend Lagoinha and have no connection to the case.

The money trail

Each step is attributed by the press to Coaf or Federal Police reports; we did not have full access to the documents. A Coaf report does not prove a crime: it flags inconsistent transactions, which the police investigate.

  1. 1 · Origin

    Funds linked to Master

    The Federal Police says Zettel received R$190.2 million in profits from the FIP Kairós fund in 2023 and 2024, and describes the fund as part of a laundering structure linked to the bank.

    R$190.2 M
  2. 2 · The operator

    Fabiano Zettel

    Between June 2021 and January 2022, his personal account moved R$99.2 million, about 215 times seven months of his declared monthly income of R$66,000 (R$462,000). Transactions include transfers and are not the same as income. For Coaf, it may have been used to move third parties' money.

    R$99.2 M in 7 months
  3. 3 · The church

    Lagoinha Belvedere

    Zettel presided over the congregation and was responsible for the account. He, Vorcaro's father and ex-wife, and the man called "Sicário" made 37 deposits. Folha counted 54 transfers from Zettel, totaling R$40.9 million.

    R$19.4 M from family and allies
  4. 4 · Destination

    Construction and events

    Most of the money went out to construction companies, engineering firms, metal structures and event producers. The construction companies alone received R$4.9 million.

    R$28.6 M in outflows

Who is who

Fabiano Zettel

Open

Vorcaro's brother-in-law. Volunteer pastor and president of Lagoinha Belvedere.

  • Married Natália Vorcaro, Daniel's sister, in 2018, in a ceremony officiated by André Valadão.
  • Came from the Bola de Neve church to Lagoinha in 2024 and led the construction of the Belvedere temple.
  • His declared wealth went from R$67.5 million in 2021 to R$204.3 million in 2024. Jewelry and watches add up to R$50.9 million.
  • He was the largest individual donor to Tarcísio's campaign in 2022, with R$2 million, and gave R$3 million to Bolsonaro's.
  • Removed by the church in November 2025 and arrested in March 2026.

Lagoinha

Documented

The institution. We found no formal public investigation against the institution in the sources cited.

  • The Belvedere temple opened in September 2024, with 14,400 m² and rent of about R$420,000 a month, aimed at business people and Belo Horizonte's elite.
  • The congregation closed on March 15, 2026, days after Zettel's arrest.
  • Vorcaro hosted a music program on Rede Super, a broadcaster linked to the church.
  • Clava Forte Bank, a fintech linked to Lagoinha, closed after Vorcaro's first arrest. A deputy asked the INSS CPMI to investigate it.

What they sayEach congregation manages its own money. It was told the funds were for construction, which did take place, and it did not know about the volume. It says it has no institutional link to Master.

What is strange and what is explained

What is in the records

  • The Belvedere congregation's account moved 60 times its presumed revenue in one year, with money from the Vorcaro family.
  • Zettel's declared wealth tripled between 2021 and 2024.
  • Nikolas had direct contact with Vorcaro and called him "Dani", after saying he did not remember talking to him.
  • In 2024, at Vorcaro's request, Valadão asked Nikolas not to attack him without talking first. Vorcaro said he had paid for the deputy's flights.

What is not yet proven

  • That Lagoinha, as an institution, laundered money or knew the origin of the funds.
  • That Valadão played a role in Master's business.
  • That Nikolas received cash payments or an illicit advantage from Vorcaro. The use of the aircraft is a reported logistical benefit, whose legality requires its own analysis.
  • That Zettel's donations to the 2022 campaigns were bribes. The claim is in a Vorcaro plea deal proposal that was rejected.

Porchat's video

In September 2026, Fábio Porchat published the episode "Grandes Igrejas, Master Negócios" (Big Churches, Master Deals), from the Revelando series of the Calma Urgente podcast. It reached the top of YouTube's trending videos in less than a day. Its thesis is that Vorcaro acted as a financier of figures in Bolsonaro's movement, using business and church networks, and that Nikolas is one of the main beneficiaries. Porchat says the target is not the faith of churchgoers.

The video presents Nikolas as a Lagoinha churchgoer. The records say otherwise: the church states he is not a member, and he attends his father's church. What links Nikolas to Lagoinha is his friendship with André Valadão. For the money links, this page only uses reports and news stories.

Diario de Pernambuco

The church, in context

Lagoinha was founded in 1957, in Belo Horizonte, as a traditional Baptist church. From 1972, with pastor Márcio Valadão, it adopted a charismatic style and grew from about 300 members to a network with hundreds of temples in Brazil and abroad. With André Valadão came network expansion, coaching techniques and what critics call dominion theology.

This style is influenced by American churches, like much of Brazilian Pentecostalism since 1910. This history helps understand the church, but has nothing to do with the Master case. What links Lagoinha to the case are the people and the money described above.
