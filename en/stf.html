The Supreme Court
in the case

Four justices appear in Federal Police reports or in Vorcaro's messages. Justices can only be investigated with authorization from the Supreme Court's own full bench, and to date no investigation has been opened.

The September 15 session

It was the first time the full court considered opening an investigation into one of its own justices. After more than six hours, nobody voted on the merits.

Agenda

Chief Justice Fachin scheduled the session only for the Federal Police report on Moraes and refused to join the case with Moraes's petition against Mendonça.

Point of order

Justice Gilmar Mendes asked for the two cases to be judged together. The vote stayed on that procedural point. Gilmar, Dino, Zanin and Moraes himself voted to join them.

Recused

Toffoli declared himself recused. Prosecutor General Paulo Gonet, also cited in the messages, said he had not been close to Vorcaro.

Result

Justice Dino asked for more time to review. Fachin announced a partial 4 to 3 tally for keeping the cases separate. The court neither validated nor annulled the Federal Police report.

What still has to happen

Investigating Moraes

Depends on the case returning from review and on a majority of the full court. In a tie, the result most favorable to the person under investigation prevails.

Impeachment

There are dozens of requests against Moraes in the Senate. Senate president Davi Alcolumbre ruled out moving them forward and said he will wait for the Supreme Court.

Plea deal

No proposal from Vorcaro has been ratified. The final word belongs to Justice Mendonça, who requires new facts and full compensation.

Testimony

Vorcaro's new testimony to the Federal Police was left for after the elections, at the defense's request.
