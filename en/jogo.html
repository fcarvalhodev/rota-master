CDB Tower

You own a small bank. To grow, you sell CDBs (bank deposit certificates) paying far more than everyone else. Each CDB becomes a floor of the tower. How high can it go?

How to play: drop each block on top of the previous one. Whatever hangs over the edge falls off, and the tower gets narrower. Facts from the real story appear as you climb.
