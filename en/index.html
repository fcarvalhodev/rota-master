Flávio and the "Dark Horse" money, step by step

Flávio's link to Vorcaro's money is proven. What is not yet proven is whether there was a crime. These are different questions, and each one has its own label.

1

He asked for the money. In an audio released by The Intercept in May 2026, Flávio presses Vorcaro for the film's instalments about his father. The deal foresaw about US$ 24 million, close to R$ 134 million, in 14 instalments. Flávio confirms the request and says it was private sponsorship. Documented

2

The money arrived. Bank receipts show at least US$ 10.6 million, about R$ 61 million, paid by May 2025. The route was Entre Investimentos, then the Havengate fund in Texas, and finally Go Up, the film's production company. Documented

3

Where did it come from and where did it go? It is not known whether the money was Vorcaro's own or came from Master's coffers. The owner of Entre signed a plea deal with the Prosecutor General's Office and said he made transfers and cash deliveries at Vorcaro's request; the Supreme Court has not yet approved it. The Federal Police are looking into whether part of the money paid for Eduardo Bolsonaro's life in the US instead of the film. Open

4

Was there public money? In September 2026, Operation Make Up, by the Federal Police and the Comptroller General's Office, investigated R$ 2 million in budget amendments from congressman Mario Frias to an institute owned by the producer. Go Up received R$ 750,000 from another company, but the police say it is not proven that it came from the amendments. Flávio says there was "zero" public money. Neither side is proven. Open

Where the proof ends

The case reaches the Supreme Court, the government, the opposition and the Centrão (the big-tent centrist parties). But the level of proof is very different from one name to another.

What is proven

  • The Central Bank liquidated Master in November 2025, and Vorcaro was arrested on his way to Malta.
  • Lula received Vorcaro at the presidential palace on December 4, 2024, off the official schedule. Guido Mantega, who worked for the banker, arranged it.
  • Weeks earlier, in October 2024, Vorcaro ordered a whisky worth about R$ 500,000 and wrote "I need to give it to Lula", according to messages on his phone.
  • Flávio Bolsonaro asked Vorcaro for money for the film "Dark Horse" and confirms it. At least R$ 61 million reached the production company.
  • Flávio has been under STF investigation since October 2026 on suspicion of money laundering linked to Willer Tomaz. The Federal Police found a hidden room with a money-counting machine in Tomaz's hangar, with no money inside.
  • Nikolas Ferreira flew on Vorcaro's plane during the 2022 campaign.
  • Justice Mendonça received Vorcaro in March 2025, before becoming rapporteur.
  • Justice Toffoli stepped down as rapporteur after his family's resort's link to a fund of Vorcaro's brother-in-law became public.

What is not yet proven

  • That Moraes acted as a justice in Vorcaro's favor. The Supreme Court has not yet decided whether to open an investigation.
  • The allowances to Ciro Nogueira and the payments linked to Jaques Wagner. Both deny them.
  • That the "Dark Horse" money went to Eduardo Bolsonaro, that it came from the Master fraud, or that budget amendments reached the film.
  • That Flávio is a hidden partner in Best Bet or received money from Willer Tomaz's network, and that the hangar room held cash.
  • That Lula received the whisky. The Planalto denies it, and the only clue is a lawyer's message.
  • That Lula did anything for Master. We found no public investigation against him in the sources cited as of the date of this review.
  • Any link between Lulinha and Vorcaro or Master. None was found in the public sources cited.
